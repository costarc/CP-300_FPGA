DISCLAIMER - USE AT YOUR OWN RISK
=================================

Installing the CP-300 gateware means REFLASHING THE BOARD. The FPGA bitstream, the device tree overlay and the
Hart Software Services bootloader in the BeagleV-Fire's flash are overwritten. The board's own update mechanism keeps
no fallback copy.

A failed or interrupted flash, a power loss during the update, a defect or incompatibility in this software or
gateware, or a different board or software version than the one it was tried on can leave the board unable to start
("bricked"). Recovering such a board may need extra hardware (a FlashPro5/6 programmer) and skills, and may not be
possible at all.
